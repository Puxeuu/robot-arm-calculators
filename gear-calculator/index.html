How it works

Model: the DC motor follows a straight torque-speed line from stall torque (zero speed) to no-load speed (zero torque). A gearbox of ratio i with efficiency η gives Tout = Tmotor·i·η and nout = nmotor/i. The efficiency is applied to torque only.

Operating point: for a load torque TL the motor torque is TL/(i·η) and its speed is n0·(1 − Tmotor/Tstall). Power out = TL·ωout.

Ratio you need: i = T·SF / (Tcont·η). Use the continuous torque of the motor, not the stall torque, or it will overheat. Take the torque from the joint torque calculator.

Not included: friction, inertia, backlash, thermal limits and supply voltage sag. Units: N·m, rpm, W.
